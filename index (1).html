How one order is wired — and where the thread breaks

This is the question that decides whether the ERP is a system or a filing cabinet: when an order moves from pre-sales to production, what travels with it and on which field? Below is every hand-off in the chain, the exact field that carries it, and whether that field is populated in your data.

In the guide, the order lives in two things: a Sales Order (the contract) and a Project (the folder). In this ERP there are 39 sales orders and 0 projects. The folder the whole method depends on has never been created, so "cost, WIP and profit for one order" cannot be filtered — there is nothing to filter on.

The threads, in the order value travels

Commercial thread: what the buyer agreed and what we will be paid. Execution thread: what the factory must make, by when, from which material. They meet on one value — the style × colour × size SKU (a variant Item in this ERP) — and they are held together by three link fields.

Commercial thread — what was agreed and what we get paid Buyer inquiry→ Cost sheet→ Quotation→ Sales contractbreak Sales order→ Invoice→ Payment
Execution thread — material Sales order line (SKU)→ BOM→ FR / TRbreak PO→ Receipt + rolls→ Inspection
Execution thread — make and ship Work order→ WIP · cutting · bundles→ Sewing + job cards→ Wash · finishing · packing→ Delivery note

Every hand-off, and what it turns on

"Carried on" is the literal field. LIVE means the field holds real data; HALF means the field exists but is empty or only partly populated; BROKEN means the field does not exist at all.

The one break that matters most The contract number the business runs on — CTXG-YYYY-MM-### — is supposed to appear on the fabric requisition, the trim requisition, the WIP line and the production card. In this ERP that string exists on three unrelated places: as free text custom_source_contract_no on 10 purchase orders, as free text source_contract_no on 423 fabric rolls, and as the document name of the one Sales Contract. None of them is a link, and none of them is the format the merchandiser was told to use.

Why that matters for the dry run "Auto-generate FR and TR from the sales contract" and "auto-populate the WIP from the contract" are the two efficiency targets named in the meeting. In this ERP neither can be a database trigger. They are either a button a merchandiser presses in the browser, or an external job that writes through the API. Both are buildable; neither is automatic in the way a standard ERP add-on would be.
