## Notes
- All eight screens render without errors; none returned `retired`/`live:false`. The problem is meaning, not crashes. Root cause: `_snapshot()` in backend/functions.py (lines 363-606) and rulebook/fabric logic were written for the old KOGO ERP data model; roughly 12 of the doctypes they read do not exist on the Arinox site, and BOM/Work Order exist but are empty because Arinox uses Sales Contract / Cost Sheet / PP Meeting / Packing List instead.
- Small change candidates: (1) Fabric price check, Forecast: repoint packed qty to Packing List and consumption to the Arinox costing/BOM doctype, or hide the screen until then. (2) Risks/Actions: filter out rule keys that depend on missing doctypes (BOM_MISSING_ORDER, Per-size BOM coverage, no Work Order) via a doctype-exists gate in rulebook.py; the remaining delivery/overdue/invoice rules then give honest output. (3) Operations charts: hide the 4 empty charts.
- Replace by agent layer (AGENT_ARCHITECTURE.md section 3, signals/proposals): Risks, Recommended actions, Order risk overview and Forecast are the same concept as signals and proposals; keep them only as a thin read until that lands rather than investing in the legacy rulebook. Fabric price check is a playbook candidate (costing vs PO rate vs BOM).
- Keep as is: Order intake (real queue, but verify approve path separately) and Activity log (retitle as "Tower activity").
